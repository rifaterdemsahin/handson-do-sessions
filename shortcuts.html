⌨️ Complete Shortcut Suite

Zero-mouse studio control. Six system-level shortcuts keep your hands on the keyboard and your eyes on the lens while recording the Hands-On DO vault.

1. The Complete Shortcut Suite

Every shortcut below is registered at the system level in OBS Studio, so it fires even when OBS is hidden behind VS Code, a terminal, or your browser. The on-screen HUD confirms every action without you looking away from the lens.

Shortcut Action On-Screen Confirmation
⌥ Option + 1 Start / Stop Recording 🔴 Recording Started / ⏹️ Recording Stopped
⌥ Option + 2 Screen Scene 🖥️ Screen Scene
⌥ Option + 3 Camera Scene 📷 Camera Scene
⌥ Option + 4 Open Movies Folder 📁 Movies Folder (Finder)
⌥ Option + 5 Copy Last Recording ✅ Copied to Clipboard + File Name
⌥ Option + 6 Show / Close Preview 👁️ Preview Shown / 🙈 Preview Closed
⌥ Option + 7 Move TV Projector (Samsung) 📺 Projector on TV / ⚠️ Failed
🎬

2. The Camera Scene & Screen Scene on the MacBook Pro

The MacBook Pro studio runs two primary scenes, each one keystroke away. They map directly to the Talking Head and Full Screen modes used in the OBS scene architecture.

📷 Camera Scene — ⌥ + 3

Full-screen Sony ZV-1 / Insta360 feed on the MacBook Pro. Used for hooks, the thought-process narration, transitions, and lesson wrap-ups. Framing is verified on the TV confidence monitor before going live.

🖥️ Screen Scene — ⌥ + 2

Full-screen macOS display capture for hands-on coding, terminal logs, and repository walkthroughs. Toggle back to the camera scene the instant you need eye contact again.

  • Why two scenes: One keystroke separates "teaching with your face" from "teaching with your code", so you never break flow to hunt through OBS panels on camera.
  • System-level hotkeys: Registered in the OBS profile files (basic.ini and cleanslate.json) so they work globally on the MacBook Pro.
  • HUD feedback: A floating pill and audio cue confirms the active scene instantly — no need to glance at the OBS window.

Full setup log — Insta360 install, HUD indicator, LaunchAgent, 5-minute recording splits and the TV confidence monitor — is documented in Step 03: OBS Implementations & Scenes.

3. Building Muscle Memory Before You Record

Shortcuts only pay off if they are automatic. Rehearse the six keys for two minutes before every take until the sequence lives in your fingers:

  • Scene rehearsals: ⌥+3 (camera) → ⌥+2 (screen) → ⌥+3 (camera). Repeat five times without looking down.
  • Recording drills: ⌥+1 to start, talk for ten seconds, ⌥+1 to stop. Verify the HUD confirms both transitions.
  • Export habit: Finish a take, hit ⌥+5, paste the file straight into Slack, Skool, or your editor for review.

⚡ The Shortcut Rule of the Studio

If an action takes longer than one keystroke, it will eventually stop happening mid-session. Keep the control surface friction-free:

1. Hands Stay on Keys

Never mouse-hunt in OBS while teaching. The camera sees everything.

2. Confirm Without Looking

Trust the HUD pill and the audio cue to confirm scene and recording state.

3. One Take, One Flow

Stop stumbling over controls; stay inside the explanation and the code.

4. Rehearse Weekly

Two minutes of drift rehearsal before each shoot keeps the muscle memory sharp.
