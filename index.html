🏠 Studio Setup & Room Environment Protocol

Filming in a flat requires proactive environment management to ensure a pristine, professional visual frame and clean audio floor.

🧹 Flat & Room Tidy-Up Protocol

Clean backdrop creates immediate visual authority:

  • Clear Visual Clutter: Put away laundry, clothes racks, mugs, cables, and loose paperwork behind the chair.
  • Camera Framing Check: Check what the Sony ZV-1 lens actually sees; keep the background aesthetic minimal and intentional.
  • Desk Surface: Remove rogue cups, loose adapters, and anything reflective that catches glare.

💡 Lighting & Visual Calibration

Flat room lighting control without unpredictable sunlight:

  • Close Blinds/Curtains: Eliminate shifting daylight that ruins color consistency between takes.
  • Face Lighting: Key light positioned 45° to illuminate face brighter than the background wall.
  • Camera Settings: Sony ZV-1 on Manual exposure, Auto Focus Continuous (AF-C), shutter speed 1/60s (or 1/120s for 60fps), clean aperture.

🔇 Room Acoustics & Noise Isolation

Elgato Mic & Scarlett 2i2 noise floor management:

  • Kill Appliance Hum: Temporarily pause robot vacuums, noisy fans, heaters, or AC units before filming.
  • Phone & Notifications: Put phone on Do Not Disturb / Silent; mute desktop notifications on the Mac M1.
  • Mic Proximity: Keep the Elgato mic 4-6 inches from your mouth so your voice drowns out any subtle flat room echo.

⚙️ Hardware Routing & OBS Studio Configuration

Optimized for Apple Silicon hardware acceleration, zero frame drops, and crash-proof recording.

📹 Video Routing (Sony ZV-1)

  • Zero-Latency Route: Micro-HDMI from Sony ZV-1 → Elgato Cam Link 4K (or HDMI-to-USB capture card) → Mac M1 hub/port.
  • Continuous Power: Dummy battery (AC adapter) or USB continuous power plugged into wall to eliminate mid-take shutdowns.
  • Clean HDMI Out: Enable Clean HDMI display on ZV-1 so camera UI icons don't leak into OBS.

🎙️ Audio Routing (Elgato + Scarlett 2i2)

  • XLR Setup (Wave DX): Mic → XLR cable → Scarlett 2i2 Input 1 → USB-C → Mac M1. Turn on 48V phantom power if condenser.
  • Gain Staging: Dial Scarlett halo gain until it stays solid green and flashes yellow on peaks (never red).
  • Alternative USB (Wave:3): Direct USB-C into Mac M1 with Elgato Wave Link digital routing.

💻 Mac M1 Hub & OBS Encoding

  • Encoder: Apple VT H264 Hardware Encoder (harnesses M1 media engine, sub-5% CPU usage).
  • Format & Bitrate: Record in MKV (crash-resilient). Remux to MP4 later in OBS. Bitrate 15,000–25,000 Kbps at 60 FPS.
  • Resolution: Canvas 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K (3840x2160) matching your monitor aspect ratio.

🎥 Background Visuals & What's On The Footage

Creating a cinematic, professional broadcast frame in a flat room, and calibrating exactly what your viewers see on every take.

✨ Optical Bokeh & Camera Separation

How to make a flat bedroom look like a million-dollar studio:

  • Aperture (f/1.8 – f/2.8): Open the Sony ZV-1 lens wide to create genuine optical depth of field. This blurs flat walls and shelf clutter into creamy, buttery bokeh.
  • Subject Distance: Sit 2.5 to 3 feet from the lens, while leaving 4 to 6 feet between your chair and the back wall. Distance creates separation.
  • Framing & Eye Line: Align your eyes along the upper horizontal third of the frame. Keep head room minimal (about 2-3 inches above your hair).

🎨 Lighting Contrast (5600K vs 2700K)

Color temperature separation turns flat rooms into dynamic sets:

  • Key Light (Face): Crisp 5600K (Daylight White) softbox/panel positioned at 45° angle, slightly above eye level. Your face should always be the brightest point in the room.
  • Background Accent (Room): Warm 2700K–3000K ambient lamp or warm LED strip behind your chair illuminating a corner or bookshelf.
  • Zero Spill: Angle the key light downward so it illuminates your face without washing out the dark, moody background wall.

🪟 Ambient & Acoustic Lock

Total environmental control before hitting Record:

  • Blackout / Blinds Closed: Block shifting UK/London outdoor daylight completely. Daylight changes exposure between takes, causing ugly visual jumps in edited cuts.
  • Zero Desk Reflections: Remove white printer paper, glass cups, or shiny watch surfaces that bounce glare into the camera sensor.
  • Cardioid Mic Positioning: Position the Elgato mic 4-6 inches from your mouth at 45° angle. High signal-to-noise ratio completely drowns out room reflections.

Scene 2 • Main Canvas + PIP

Screen Share + Picture-in-Picture

Full Screen Deep Dive

Footage Frame: 100% monitor capture. Zero camera PIP overlay.

Purpose: Dense architecture diagrams, complex multi-agent execution graphs, large terminal traces, and network payload inspections where a facecam would cover crucial text.

⚡ Tip: Hotkey assigned on Stream Deck or Mac keyboard for instant toggle.

🤖 Production Assistant Protocol: Getting In Front of the Camera

Your step-by-step assistant guide to eliminating procrastination, cutting setup friction, and hitting Record in under 7 minutes.

Step 1: The "Lens-Only" Declutter Rule

Do not tidy the entire flat before filming. That is procrastination disguised as productivity. Turn on Sony ZV-1 monitor. If laundry or a cup is NOT in the frame, ignore it. If it IS in the frame, shove it behind the camera tripod. Done in 3 minutes.

Step 2: The 3 Power Switches

Flip three switches in order: (1) Main power strip (powers Mac M1, Scarlett 2i2, ZV-1 dummy battery). (2) Key light + ambient lamp. (3) Launch OBS. In under 90 seconds, your entire hardware pipeline is hot and active.

Step 3: The 5-Second Seat-Down Rule

Do not wait to "feel in the mood". Count down 5-4-3-2-1, slide into the chair, pull the Elgato mic 4 inches away, verify the Scarlett halo flashes green, and immediately hit Start Recording. Action produces adrenaline; waiting produces anxiety.

Step 4: The Never-Stop Stumble Rule

If you stumble over a sentence, make a coding typo, or the AI hallucinates: DO NOT STOP RECORDING. Pause for 2 seconds, take a breath, repeat the phrase or solve the bug live. Real engineering builds deep trust with your community.
