🧠 The "Age of AI" Thought Process

Coding in the age of AI isn't about blind generation—it is about rigorous architectural specification, tool routing, and agentic orchestration.

01

Decomposition & Spec Architecture

Moving from vague vibes to deterministic task decomposition. Structuring requirements into verifiable atomic increments that AI models can execute without context drift.

02

Tool Crafting & Clear Boundaries

Equipping models with resilient, schema-validated tools. Eliminating tool sprawl and hallucination through semantic routing, strict JSON validation, and bounded interfaces.

03

Deterministic Verification & Evals

Pair programming with AI where human engineering intuition governs correctness. Running live test suites, visual checks, and behavioral evals at every turn.

04

Resilient Multi-Agent Orchestration

Coordinating autonomous subagent pools, stateful handoffs, error escalation policies, and self-healing pipelines that gracefully absorb API hiccups.

🏠 Studio Setup & Room Environment Protocol

Filming in a flat requires proactive environment management to ensure a pristine, professional visual frame and clean audio floor.

🧹 Flat & Room Tidy-Up Protocol

Clean backdrop creates immediate visual authority:

  • Clear Visual Clutter: Put away laundry, clothes racks, mugs, cables, and loose paperwork behind the chair.
  • Camera Framing Check: Check what the Sony ZV-1 lens actually sees; keep the background aesthetic minimal and intentional.
  • Desk Surface: Remove rogue cups, loose adapters, and anything reflective that catches glare.

💡 Lighting & Visual Calibration

Flat room lighting control without unpredictable sunlight:

  • Close Blinds/Curtains: Eliminate shifting daylight that ruins color consistency between takes.
  • Face Lighting: Key light positioned 45° to illuminate face brighter than the background wall.
  • Camera Settings: Sony ZV-1 on Manual exposure, Auto Focus Continuous (AF-C), shutter speed 1/60s (or 1/120s for 60fps), clean aperture.

🔇 Room Acoustics & Noise Isolation

Elgato Mic & Scarlett 2i2 noise floor management:

  • Kill Appliance Hum: Temporarily pause robot vacuums, noisy fans, heaters, or AC units before filming.
  • Phone & Notifications: Put phone on Do Not Disturb / Silent; mute desktop notifications on the Mac M1.
  • Mic Proximity: Keep the Elgato mic 4-6 inches from your mouth so your voice drowns out any subtle flat room echo.

⚙️ Hardware Routing & OBS Studio Configuration

Optimized for Apple Silicon hardware acceleration, zero frame drops, and crash-proof recording.

📹 Video Routing (Sony ZV-1)

  • Zero-Latency Route: Micro-HDMI from Sony ZV-1 → Elgato Cam Link 4K (or HDMI-to-USB capture card) → Mac M1 hub/port.
  • Continuous Power: Dummy battery (AC adapter) or USB continuous power plugged into wall to eliminate mid-take shutdowns.
  • Clean HDMI Out: Enable Clean HDMI display on ZV-1 so camera UI icons don't leak into OBS.

🎙️ Audio Routing (Elgato + Scarlett 2i2)

  • XLR Setup (Wave DX): Mic → XLR cable → Scarlett 2i2 Input 1 → USB-C → Mac M1. Turn on 48V phantom power if condenser.
  • Gain Staging: Dial Scarlett halo gain until it stays solid green and flashes yellow on peaks (never red).
  • Alternative USB (Wave:3): Direct USB-C into Mac M1 with Elgato Wave Link digital routing.

💻 Mac M1 Hub & OBS Encoding

  • Encoder: Apple VT H264 Hardware Encoder (harnesses M1 media engine, sub-5% CPU usage).
  • Format & Bitrate: Record in MKV (crash-resilient). Remux to MP4 later in OBS. Bitrate 15,000–25,000 Kbps at 60 FPS.
  • Resolution: Canvas 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K (3840x2160) matching your monitor aspect ratio.
