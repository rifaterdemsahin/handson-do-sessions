🛑 The Old Model (Killed)

The 1-on-1 Ephemeral Bottleneck

Original Skool announcement that created massive administrative friction and zero durable assets:

1-1 Workshop for Beginners 🌐 Monday, September 14th @ 7pm - 9pm London time https://www.skool.com/live/rjc5cndHp3L 🚨 URGENT NOTICE: Please select from the presets and leave a comment at least 12 hours before we meet! ⏰ 👇 https://www.skool.com/delivery-pilot-8938/1-1-workshops 🌐 Online ( Remote ) Audience : Students / Startups / Nocode 📹🚫 (Video not recorded)
  • 12-Hour Urgent Barrier: Demanding presets and comments 12 hours in advance scared away hesitant beginners.
  • Performance Anxiety: Being trapped 1-on-1 with an instructor on video creates intense social stress.
  • 📹🚫 Zero Compounding: Value evaporated into thin air the instant the call disconnected.
  • Single Point of Failure: If the attendee had an emergency or flaked, 2 hours of founder energy was wasted.

Why We Had to Make This Pivot

When starting a community, founders intuitively offer 1-on-1 sessions because it feels personal and attentive. But in practice, 1-on-1 unrecorded sessions suffer from catastrophic systemic flaws:

1. It Punishes the Shy & Intimidated: The very people who need the most help with AI anxiety are the least likely to book a 1-on-1 call. They worry they will look foolish or won't know how to fill out the "preset options 12 hours in advance."

2. The Ephemeral Treadmill: If you teach Git commands or Docker configs to one student in an unrecorded session, you have to repeat the exact same explanation to the next student tomorrow. You are trading your life energy for zero leverage.

3. The Guilt Cycle: If someone misses their 1-on-1 slot, both the founder and the member feel guilty. Rescheduling turns into endless DM tennis.

By pivoting to 100% recorded, categorized sessions, we removed all guilt, eliminated administrative hurdles, and created an asset that works for our community 24/7/365.

📚

The 3-Tier Curriculum Structure

In the new model, every single DO session uploaded to Skool and YouTube is strictly categorized into one of three distinct progressive tiers:

Level 1 • Beginner

Deconstruction & Spec Architecture

For those feeling paralyzed by AI velocity. Mental models, environment bootstrapping, terminal CLI agents, and transforming vague prompts into deterministic specs.

repo: task-decomposition
Level 2 • Intermediate

Tool Routing & Guardrails

Equipping autonomous agents with resilient tools. Vector embedding routing, boundary testing suites, and live agentic pair programming with test evals.

repo: semantic-tool-routing
Level 3 • Advanced

Resilient Multi-Agent Pools

Coordinating autonomous subagent pools, stateful handoffs, error escalation policies, self-healing pipelines, and programmatic video rendering.

repo: resilient-subagent-pool
